## Business Goals

1. **Reduce Time-to-Market**: Decrease average onboarding time for new services from weeks to under an hour.
2. **Increase Operational Stability**: Target MTTR (Mean Time to Resolution) of under 15 minutes via unified alerting and pre-packaged runbooks.
3. **Automate Compliance Auditability**: Zero security alerts during external compliance checks through strict OPA admission policies and secure-by-design templates.
4. **Reduce Infrastructure Overhead**: Consolidate Kubernetes cluster administration under common GitOps controls.
